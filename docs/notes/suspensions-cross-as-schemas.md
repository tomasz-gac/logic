A table entry cannot hold a live suspension: the driver treats
suspensions specially for the agenda fixpoint to be safe, so they must
never become store or theory citizens (the human's ruling, Aug 2026,
which killed suspensions-as-factors). What CAN cross is a DESCRIPTION:
the watched surface, the wake predicate, and the body, all rebuilt
positionally from a schema carried since minting — and on the far side
the description re-enters through Propagation.suspend itself, the
driver's own door, in the consumer's scope. The ring holds syntax; the
driver holds the citizen, on both sides.

Identity cannot ride the lambdas — lambda equality is unreliable, and
without a working equality even a == a dies at the first renaming: ⊗
loses idempotence (the same pending condition from two combined regions
re-parks twice — duplicate work, and a double-count under weighted
semantics) and regions that should merge never do. The propagator
answer transfers whole: a crossable suspension is NAMED, identity =
(name, watched actuals), predicate and body excluded, name determines
semantics. Naming and schema-ification are ONE requirement: a mint site
that can carry (template, actuals) is exactly a site whose name can
determine the template, and the anonymous lambda that cannot be named
is the same one that cannot rebuild — one eligibility rule, no
crossable citizen with unreliable equality.

In the ring, a crossed suspension is one more conjunct of a region with
structural comparison over that identity: two DISTINCT pending
computations are incomparable by construction, so ⊗ accumulates them
(collapsing only true duplicates) and ⊕'s absorption never fires
across a suspension-bearing region. A region carrying one is not final
(never reaches 1 in the cell) until the consumer resolves it — the
conditional-answer reading of "this answer holds provided the pending
goal completes". The crossings behave right without new machinery:
about() is allowed lossy, so suspensions never enter keys (dropping
widens the key — the sound direction); all() pulls everything, so they
ride answers whole, like existential witnesses; split() never claims
them because a pending body does not decompose over names. At
consumption, the trial reads the description three ways before
re-parking — the nogood verification core's reading, reused, not the
disjunctive store resurrected: the OR already lives in ⊕, and a woken
body that branches flows through ordinary search into multiple regions.

What it buys: answers may leave a tabled body carrying their pending
conditions as data, relaxing #68's refusal exactly where it can be made
safe — the tabled REST FactSource (unbound IN arg suspends) is the
workload that needs this and the trigger for building it. What it does
NOT buy: closures that cross unschema'd (they keep refusing), any
subsumption between pending work, or key-side use.

Cheapest kill: the schema at Projection's mint sites turns out unable to
express a real wake predicate's context (some predicate is genuinely
non-positional) — then the crossing covers a subset too small to pay for
its machinery, and #68's refusal was already the right answer.
