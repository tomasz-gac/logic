## The observation (banked, commits to nothing)

A literal denotes a region; the scratch denotes a region; the trial's
verdicts are order theory:

    disjoint (meet is bottom)  →  REFUTED
    scratch ⊑ literal (leq)    →  ENTAILED
    otherwise                  →  OWED

One concept, two witnesses: Substitutions answers through the unifier
(whose residual prefix is a remainder no generic meet yields); lattice
values would answer through (meet, leq, isAbsorbing) — the pair the
cargo-typed chassis proposed, right algebra, wrong seat. A Prefix is
the one-branch compressed store. The same classification already runs,
independently built, in Condition's antichain insert, tabling's
entailment matching, and weighted grouping — a correspondence worth
knowing, not yet a refactor worth doing.
