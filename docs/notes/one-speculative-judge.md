# Doomed and entailed are one judgment — extract the speculative optimizer

- **status**: argued, recorded on the human's instruction (Sep 4) — not
  built; opens as a design pass on the constraint core when taken up.
- **evidence held**: derivation. Death and rank are conflated in one
  number today: `estimate` serves ordering (statistical, may only cost
  steps when wrong) AND the doom reading (`estimate == 0` ⇒ hoist
  failure — a REFUTATION claim that changes answers when wrong, i.e. a
  trust surface in #107's sense). The refutation is semantically a
  scratch imposition against imperfect knowledge, read the trial's way:
  fail = REFUTED, unchanged = ENTAILED, new = OWED. Logic's optimizer
  already reads REFUTED at the pricing seat through the trial; pldb's
  table records hand-roll it as bucket statistics — approximations
  whose correctness is promised, not computed. The claim: one budgeted
  component owns the judgment — impose-on-scratch, answer
  REFUTED | ENTAILED | OWED — and every consumer reads verdicts instead
  of asserting them. Three consumers exist already: the doom check
  (derived instead of promised — the trust surface becomes a proof
  surface), the plan-time entailed-conjunct skip
  (entailed-conjunct-skip.md), and commit-time entailment selection in
  the posted table's verdict ladder (drop the discharge conde when a
  disjunct is already the case — the disjunctive store's discharge
  doctrine). Monotone stability makes the verdicts cacheable: knowledge
  only grows within a branch, so REFUTED and ENTAILED are permanent
  once judged; only OWED can move.
- **imports**: the trial's three-way verdicts, the pricing seat, #107
  (bounds are trust surfaces), #116 (the imposition law kit —
  entailed-exact is the licensing law).
- **obligations**: (1) size the trial's public face — "impose this
  posting/residue bundle on scratch, answer three ways" as an operation
  pldb can reach (constraint core, STOP-listed, design-first); (2) the
  cost discipline: statistics stay the fast path (empty bucket ⇒
  REFUTED without imposing; the judge arbitrates only when statistics
  say "maybe"), judging runs at posting time and commit points, never
  per wake — the discipline that separates this from the disjunction
  store's per-examination trial storm; (3) the oracle pair: a
  doomed-derivation receipt (hand-rolled doom deleted, answers
  identical) and the entailment-selection receipt ({≠2}⊕{≠3} at a
  ground anchor discharging to one answer through the direct path);
  (4) a name for the component, ratified in conversation. Cheapest
  kill: the judge's imposition cost exceeding its pruning value on the
  step-count pins — the ambient optimizer's +37% unfold tax (#147) is
  the standing precedent for an always-on rewriter charging more than
  it earns.
- **links**: entailed-conjunct-skip.md (the ENTAILED reading at the
